AIR MAIL

My dearest Constance,

Happy Birthday, my love. Even in the middle of busy lectures, deadlines, and exam revision, my favourite part of every single day is still talking to you, hearing your voice, seeing your smile, and knowing I get to call you mine.

When I sat down to make this little gift for you, I realised that a store-bought card would never have enough space for everything I wanted to tell you. There are so many small, quiet moments with you that I replay in my head when I am supposed to be studying—the way your eyes crinkle when you laugh at a terrible joke, the way you instinctively reach for my hand when we are walking side by side, and the calm that settles over my whole chest the second you hug me.

You bring so much gentleness, warmth, and quiet magic into my life. Whether we are dressed up for a proper dinner date, getting caught in the rain on a coffee run, or just sharing late-night snacks in hoodies while we quiz each other on lecture notes, being around you always feels like coming home. You have this rare, effortless way of making the people you love feel deeply understood and completely safe.

I know how hard you work and how much heart you pour into everything you care about, even on the days when you feel tired or stretched thin. I want you to know how proud I am of the person you are—not just for everything you achieve, but for your kindness, your patience, your softness, and the way you light up every room you walk into (especially when you are wearing your favourite blue).

So I built this little blue-and-cream scrapbook website—and gathered the love letters I have written for you over time in the archive next door—so you have a permanent little corner on the internet that belongs only to us. Whenever you have a long day, whenever you miss me, or whenever you just want to be reminded of how cherished you are, you can open this link on your phone and read every word back.

I hope this new year of your life brings you every bit of joy, peace, and magic that you bring into my world every single day. Happy Birthday, my darling.

Forever and always yours, Your Boyfriend ♡

Letter I — When I First Realised

From the very beginning

To the girl who stole my whole heart,

I don't think you noticed how nervous I was when I first started writing down how I felt about you. Every time we talked, I found myself wishing the conversation would never end, inventing excuses just to walk a little slower or stay a few minutes longer with you.

Even back then, it wasn't just one big thing that made me fall for you—it was a hundred tiny details all at once. It was the way your eyes light up when you talk about the things you love, the genuine kindness you show even when nobody is watching, and how effortlessly warm you make everyone around you feel.

Looking back at this first letter now, I smile because I thought I liked you so much back then—having no idea that was only the very first page, and that I would fall a hundred times deeper for you with every month that followed.

Yours from day one, Your Boyfriend ♡
